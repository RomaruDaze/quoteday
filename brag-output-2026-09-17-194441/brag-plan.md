# Brag Plan: QuoteDay (Apple, 2026)

## What is this app?
An Android app that sends you one of your own saved quotes every day, as a notification and on a calm Today card. Warm "Japandi" palette, automatic dark mode, 20 quotes free.

## The angle
We shoot a real daily-quote app the way Apple would launch a 2026 feature. Huge light type, one idea per scene, a floating device, glassy notification, slow confident motion, lots of empty space. The product is sincere, so the video is too. The only wink is how seriously a quote card gets treated.

## Hook
Near black with a warm glow. A thin giant lock-screen clock reads "7:00", then a frosted glass notification glides in: "Quote of the Day — If you get tired, learn to rest, not to quit."

## Key moments
- Today card: "In the middle of every difficulty lies opportunity. — Albert Einstein"
- Quotes list: cards settle in one by one. "Up to 20 quotes free."
- Dark mode: the phone and the world turn from cream to warm dark (the app follows the system theme through DarkAppColors).

## Outro
Logo, "QuoteDay.", "Your words, every day.", "Available on Google Play."

## User flow worth showing
Get the morning notification, open the Today card, browse your saved quotes. Dark mode is a bonus beat.

## Tone
- Preset: polished (with cinematic scale-in transitions)
- Creative direction: Apple style from 2026
- Interpretation: slow crossfades, scenes entering at 0.96 scale, light-weight SF-style type, eyebrow label plus two-line headline, sparse sound. No voice.

## Format: landscape 1920x1080 · Duration: 24s

## Visual identity
- Light: #FFFFFF→#FFF3A0→#FFDE59, surface #FFFCF0, border #E8C830, accent #B5892A (eyebrows darkened to #7D5D16 for contrast)
- Dark: #1C1910→#2E2210, surface #2A1F0E, border #5A4826, accent #CA9B38, text #FCF8EC
- Display font: SF Pro Display / -apple-system (Inter fallback). Quote text is serif italic, as in the app.

## Share copy (draft)
QuoteDay. Your words, every day. One of your saved quotes, delivered each morning. Now on Google Play.

## Audio direction
- Role: polished bed with sparse accents
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12 (110 BPM), 0.3 volume, fading out at the end
- Music cue guidance: preset vol-12. Strong cues 8.74 (phone rises), 13.11 (3rd card), 17.47 (dark mode flip), 22.93 (Google Play line). Beat grid 12.02/12.55/13.11/13.64 for the cards. Card text stays on screen after it lands, so every beat is fine to read.
- Audio-reactive: none (restraint)
- SFX: soft drop on notification, a switch on the dark-mode flip, a quiet bell on the outro.

## Storyboard
1. Hook, 0–3.82s. Dark glow, "7:00" and the date, glass notification at 1.64. Soft crossfade.
2. Reveal, 3.82–7.64s. Cream background, logo scales 0.96→1, "QuoteDay." at 4.39, "Your words, every day." at 5.34.
3. Today, 7.64–11.46s. Eyebrow "Today", headline "One quote. Picked for today.", phone rises (8.74), Today card with the Einstein quote.
4. Collection, 11.46–15.84s. Eyebrow "Your quotes", headline "Keep the words that move you.", cards cascade on beats, subline "Up to 20 quotes free."
5. Dark mode, 15.84–19.66s. Eyebrow "Dark mode", headline "Just as calm at night.", world and phone flip to dark at 17.47.
6. Outro, 19.66–24s. Dark background, logo, "QuoteDay.", tagline, "Available on Google Play." at 22.93.
